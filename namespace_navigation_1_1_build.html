◆ MakeAreaPrism()

AreaPrism Navigation::Build::MakeAreaPrism ( const Math::mat4 & boxTransform,
const Math::bbox & localBox,
const Math::mat4 & pointTransform,
const Math::vec3 * points,
SizeT pointCount )

the prism an area volume marks.

With fewer than 3 points it is the prism around the box. Otherwise the points are the outline, in order, and the height span covers every point give or take half the height of the box, so the surface a point was placed on is inside. The outline may be concave. Points are transformed by pointTransform, box corners by boxTransform
